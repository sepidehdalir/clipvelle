On-device processing

Clipvelle extracts audio locally. We do not collect your media, personal information, device identifiers or usage telemetry. The app has no account, ads, tracking SDK or upload service.

Files you choose

The app copies selected video into private application storage. Original videos remain untouched. Exported audio stays in the app’s Audio folder, accessible through Files. Removing a source removes its imported copy and keeps exports. Delete unwanted exported audio through Files. App deletion removes local app data; save important files elsewhere first.

Photos, Files and sharing

The system Photos picker shares only selected videos; broad photo-library access is not required. Your chosen Files provider may download a cloud item before import. Sharing sends a file only to the destination you choose. Those providers apply their own policies.

Purchases and local preferences

Apple handles purchases and receipts. Clipvelle uses verified StoreKit entitlement information to unlock Pro. Format/filename presets, workspace state and typed event totals remain locally on your device. We do not receive them.
