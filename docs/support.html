Importing

Choose a local video in Photos or Files. Download a cloud item first if it is unavailable. Protected, corrupt or unsupported media may not open. A video needs an audio track; an audio track can itself contain silence.

Exporting

Choose one track and a start/end range. M4A uses AAC; WAV uses uncompressed PCM. Both are44.1kHz stereo. Conversion cannot restore detail absent from the source. If storage is low, free space or try compact M4A.

Finding your audio

Exports are in Files → On My iPhone/iPad → Clipvelle → Audio. Save a copy or share to a destination of your choice. Removing a source does not delete previously exported audio.

Lifetime Pro

Single-video exports and both formats are free. Lifetime Pro unlocks batch extraction and reusable presets. Restore purchases in the Pro sheet using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Pending purchases unlock only after Apple approval.

Interruptions

Backgrounding cancels unfinished extraction safely. Return to the app and retry. Completed files remain available.
